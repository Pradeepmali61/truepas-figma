Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect biometric data (facial templates), government ID information (Passport, Driver's License, Identity Card), and account metadata to provide secure identity verification services.

2. Biometric Data Handling

Your facial template is encrypted and stored in ROC (Rank One Computing) gallery. It is never shared with third parties and is used solely for identity matching during verification.

3. Minor/Guardianship Consent

For family members under 18, a parent or legal guardian must provide explicit consent before biometric enrollment. Children aged 0-4 require document upload only — no face enrollment. Ages 5-17 require document, selfie, and face enrollment.

4. Data Retention

All data — PostgreSQL records, S3 images, and ROC face templates — is retained while your account is active and permanently deleted upon account deletion. Deletion is verified across all three systems.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to download your data, withdraw biometric consent, and delete your account at any time. Account deletion removes all data across PostgreSQL, S3, and ROC.

Terms of Service
Effective: July 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using TruePass, you agree to these Terms of Service and our Privacy Policy. TruePass is an identity verification platform that uses biometric data and government documents to verify your identity.

2. Mandatory Face Enrollment

Face enrollment is a mandatory step in the registration process. You cannot proceed to document verification or use TruePass services without completing face enrollment.

3. Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your PIN and account credentials. Face updates require PIN verification.

4. Family Member Onboarding

You may add family members under 18 as dependents. Adults 18 and older must create their own independent TruePass account. Guardianship consent is required for all minor enrollments.

5. Prohibited Uses

You may not use TruePass for fraudulent identity verification, unauthorized access, or sharing of biometric data with unauthorized parties.
